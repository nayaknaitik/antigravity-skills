# Review & Sign-Off

**Goal**: Summarize the complete discovery state for the user before generating the PRD.

**Actions**:
- Present a highly structured, concise summary of all critical decisions made during the discovery loop.
- Highlight the exact MVP scope boundaries (What is IN vs. what is OUT).
- Highlight key technical and business constraints.
- Ask the user: "Do you approve these decisions, or would you like to refine any area before I generate the final PRD?"
- Do NOT generate the PRD until explicit approval is received.
