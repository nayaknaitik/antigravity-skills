# Brainstorming & Tradeoffs

**Goal**: Help the user make informed decisions when they are uncertain or lack technical/product expertise.

**Actions**:
Whenever the user says "I don't know", "You decide", or is uncertain:
1. **Propose Alternatives**: Provide 2-3 viable options for the missing decision.
2. **Evaluate Tradeoffs**: For each option, clearly explain the pros and cons.
3. **Analyze Impact**: Highlight the business, engineering, cost, and user experience impacts of each option.
4. **Recommend**: As a Senior PM, confidently recommend the best option based on industry best practices and the established product vision.

Do not accept vague requirements. Force a decision by providing structured choices.
