# Trading Risk Auditor

## Mandatory Requirements
1. **Quality Dimension D11 (Financial Safety & Risk Control)**: Audit rules for SEC 15c3-5 pre-trade risk checks.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**: Enforcement of manual approval gates for any autonomous order execution, live deployment, or drawdown threshold modification.
3. **Code Review Scorecard**: Minimum passing audit criteria (Risk Control > 90/100, Determinism > 90/100, HITL Gates > 100%).
